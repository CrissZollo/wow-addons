# WoW addons for World of Warcraft: Forever

🇸🇪 [[Läs på svenska|Startsida]]

Addons made for our group. Each one is free to download. This wiki explains what each addon does,
what you need, and how to get it working on **Windows** and on **Linux**, where WoW runs through Wine or Proton.

## The addons

| Addon | What it does | Download (Windows) | Download (Linux) | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Herald** | Posts your first kills, boss kills, enemy players, level milestones and progress reports (gear, stats, gold) to our Discord channel | [Herald-Windows.zip](https://github.com/CrissZollo/wow-addons/releases/download/Herald-v1.0.0/Herald-Windows.zip) | [Herald-Linux.zip](https://github.com/CrissZollo/wow-addons/releases/download/Herald-v1.0.0/Herald-Linux.zip) | [[Herald]] |

Older versions are on the [releases page](https://github.com/CrissZollo/wow-addons/releases).

## Quick start

1. **Find your system's guide:** [[Install on Windows]] or [[Install on Linux]].
2. **Check what the addon needs.** Each addon's page has a *What you need* section. For example,
   Herald needs the Discord webhook link from whoever runs the channel. See [[Discord Webhook]].
3. **Download, follow the guide, restart WoW.**
4. **That's it.** New versions install themselves, see [[Automatic Updates]].
